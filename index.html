How to Connect Wirelessly via BLE:
  1. On your E-Reader, tap Menu and open the Book Library / Switch menu.
  2. Scroll down and select [+] Add More Books (BLE) to begin the 60-second pairing countdown.
  3. Click Connect E-Reader via BLE below and choose ESP32-Ereader from the browser list.
How to Connect via USB Serial:
  1. Plug your XIAO ESP32-S3 into your computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Connect USB Serial Port below and select the ESP32 CDC Modem device (115200 baud).
